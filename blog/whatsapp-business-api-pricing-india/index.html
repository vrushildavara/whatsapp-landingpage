WhatsApp Business API pricing in India (2026): what you actually pay

16 November 2026 7 min read

The WhatsApp Business API has no licence fee from Meta, but you pay per message. Meta's India rates from 1 October 2026 are ₹0.8631 for each marketing template and ₹0.115 for each utility or authentication template. Service replies to a customer who messaged you are free for the first 1,000 a month per number, then ₹0.115 each. On top of Meta's charge you pay the platform you use to send messages, either as a monthly plan or per message.

The two bills every business gets

  1. Meta's charge. A price for each message, set by Meta and the same whichever platform you use.
  2. Your platform's charge. The software that connects you to the API: a monthly plan, a markup on each message, a per-use fee, or a mix.

Most confusion about WhatsApp API cost comes from quotes that show only one of the two. Always ask for both.

Template or service message: which one are you sending?

When a customer messages you, a 24-hour window opens. Inside it you can reply with normal free-form messages; these are service replies. To message someone first, or after the window has closed, you must use a template that Meta has approved, and Meta charges for it by category. That is why a festival offer to 2,000 people costs far more than answering 2,000 questions.

What platforms add on top

  • Monthly plans: a fixed fee, often per seat or per number, whether you send ten messages or ten thousand.
  • Message markup: a few paise added to Meta's charge on every template.
  • Pay per use: no fixed fee; you pay for what the software actually does.
  • Setup fees: one-time charges for onboarding or for building the bot.

How to keep the bill down

  • Send offers to a tagged group instead of the whole list.
  • Use utility templates only for real transactions; Meta reclassifies promotional wording as marketing.
  • Reply inside the 24-hour window where you can, because service replies are free up to 1,000 a month per number.
  • Stop messaging people who never read; resend only to those who did not open the first time, once.
  • Honour STOP at once. Blocks and reports lower your quality rating, which limits how many people you can reach.

Frequently asked questions

Is the WhatsApp Business API free in India?

There is no licence fee from Meta, and service replies are free for the first 1,000 a month per number. Template messages are charged per message: ₹0.8631 for marketing and ₹0.115 for utility or authentication, at Meta's India rates from 1 October 2026. Your platform's fee is extra.

How much does one WhatsApp marketing message cost in India?

Meta charges ₹0.8631 for each marketing template delivered in India (rate from 1 October 2026). Your platform may add its own fee; Wogent adds 10 paise for a text template and 15 paise for one with an image, video or document.

Do I pay for replying to customers?

Replies inside 24 hours of the customer's last message are service replies. They are free for the first 1,000 a month per number; after that Meta charges ₹0.115 each.

Is GST charged on WhatsApp API fees?

Yes, 18% GST applies. The rates quoted here are before GST.
