WhatsApp Business API in India: what it is, how to get it, and what it costs

22 September 2026 10 min read

If you want to automate WhatsApp for your business (send bulk messages, connect a chatbot, or build an AI agent), you need the WhatsApp Business API. This guide explains what it is, how it differs from the free WhatsApp Business app, and what Indian businesses need to do to get access.

WhatsApp Business app vs WhatsApp Business API: what is the difference?

Most small businesses in India use the WhatsApp Business app: the free app you download from the Play Store or App Store. It gives you a business profile, quick replies, labels and basic automation like away messages. It works well for one or two people managing conversations manually.

The WhatsApp Business API (now called the WhatsApp Cloud API) is different. It is not an app; it is a programming interface that lets software connect to WhatsApp on behalf of your business. With the API, you can:

  • Send messages to thousands of customers at once (broadcast campaigns)
  • Connect an AI agent or chatbot that replies automatically
  • Integrate WhatsApp with your CRM, order management system or other tools
  • Give multiple team members access to a shared inbox
  • Send and receive messages programmatically, 24 hours a day

The trade-off is that the API requires a technical setup and, for most businesses, a third-party platform to manage it. You cannot use the API directly from a phone app.

WhatsApp Cloud API vs the older on-premise API

Meta originally offered the WhatsApp Business API as an on-premise solution, and businesses had to host their own server. In 2022, Meta launched the WhatsApp Cloud API, which is hosted by Meta itself. This is now the standard way to access the API.

The Cloud API is what Wogent and most modern WhatsApp automation platforms use. It is simpler to set up, does not require server management, and is updated directly by Meta.

How to get WhatsApp Business API access in India

There are two routes:

Route 1: Apply directly through Meta

You can apply for WhatsApp Business API access through Meta's Business Manager. The process involves:

  1. Creating a Meta Business Manager account and verifying your business
  2. Creating a WhatsApp Business Account (WABA) within Business Manager
  3. Adding a phone number and completing the number verification
  4. Submitting your business for Meta's review

This route is free in terms of access, but it requires technical knowledge to set up and maintain. You will also need to build or integrate the automation software yourself.

Route 2: Use a WhatsApp automation platform

Most Indian businesses go through a platform like Wogent, which handles the API setup as part of the service. The platform manages the Meta Business Manager setup, number verification and API connection on your behalf. You provide your business details and phone number; the platform handles the rest.

This is the practical route for businesses that want automation without managing technical infrastructure.

What phone number can you use?

You can use your existing business mobile number or a landline number. The number must not already be registered on the WhatsApp Business app. If it is, you will need to delete the app account first (your chat history will be lost, but your number is preserved).

Indian mobile numbers (+91) work with the WhatsApp Business API. There is no requirement to use a specific carrier.

What does the WhatsApp Business API cost?

Meta charges per message sent through the API, by category and country. In India, from 1 October 2026, a marketing template costs ₹0.8631 and a utility or authentication template ₹0.115. Service replies (anything you send inside the 24-hour window after a customer writes) are free for the first 1,000 a month per number, then ₹0.115 each. Messages customers send you are free.

Note: Meta's pricing changes periodically. Always check Meta's current published rates rather than relying on figures in any article, including this one.

WhatsApp message templates: what you need to know

When your business initiates a conversation with a customer (rather than replying to one they started), you must use a pre-approved message template. Templates are submitted to Meta for approval before use.

Templates are used for:

  • Broadcast campaigns (sending an offer to your contact list)
  • Follow-up messages to customers who have not messaged in the last 24 hours
  • Transactional notifications (order confirmations, appointment reminders)

When a customer messages you first, you can reply freely for 24 hours without using a template. This is called the "customer service window."

Is the WhatsApp Business API safe for Indian businesses?

Yes, it is the only officially sanctioned way to automate WhatsApp. Using unofficial tools (software that simulates a phone or browser session) violates WhatsApp's terms of service and risks permanent account bans.
