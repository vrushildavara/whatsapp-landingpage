WhatsApp AI agent for restaurants

Updated 13 October 2026

Wogent is a WhatsApp AI agent for Indian restaurants, cafes and cloud kitchens. It shares your menu, answers questions about dishes, timings and location, books tables and takes orders inside the WhatsApp chat, in Hindi, Gujarati or English, even during the dinner rush.

Your phone rings hardest when you're busiest

Table and order enquiries peak exactly when the kitchen and floor are full. Messages pile up, and a customer who doesn't hear back orders from someone else.

What the agent does for a restaurant

  • Menu and questions: answers from your menu: veg or Jain options, spice level, timings, location, parking.
  • Table bookings: offers a time and confirms it in the chat.
  • Orders: takes takeaway or delivery orders with items, quantity and confirmation. No form, no app.
  • Regulars: remembers returning customers, so "same as last time" works.
  • Offers: send a festival or weekend offer to your list with an approved template; the agent answers every reply.
  • Complaints go to you: an unhappy customer is handed to your team with the full chat open.

How it works

  1. Connect your WhatsApp number. Your existing number, connected through Meta's official WhatsApp Cloud API.
  2. Send us what you already have. Catalog, price list, brochure, menu or fee sheet. A PDF is fine.
  3. The agent takes the chats. We test it on real conversations first. Then it answers day and night, and your team steps in only when needed.

Frequently asked questions

Can customers book a table on WhatsApp?

Yes. The agent offers a time, takes the name and number of guests, and confirms the booking in the chat.

Can it take food orders?

Yes. It takes the order inside the WhatsApp chat with items, quantity and confirmation, from your own menu.

Does it remember regular customers?

Yes. A returning customer is greeted by name, and "same as last time" just works.

Can my staff still reply?

Yes. Every chat is in a shared inbox. Anyone on your team can take over a conversation with one tap; the agent steps back until you hand it back.

Is my WhatsApp number safe?

Yes. Wogent uses Meta's official WhatsApp Cloud API with your own business account, not unofficial automation tools. Your account still follows WhatsApp's Business and messaging policies.

How much does it cost?

There is no monthly fee. You recharge a prepaid wallet and pay per use: ₹1 per AI reply, 5 paise per utility template, 10 paise on top of Meta's charge per marketing template, and human replies are free. New accounts get 2,000 free credits. Full rate card at web.wogent.in/pricing.
