WhatsApp AI agent for travel agents and tour operators

Updated 16 November 2026

Wogent is a WhatsApp AI agent for Indian travel agents and tour operators. It answers package enquiries from your own itineraries and price sheets, collects travel dates, number of travellers and budget, and hands the ready lead to your team to quote and book, 24/7 in Hindi, Gujarati or English.

One enquiry, ten questions

"Dubai package ketla nu che?" is never one message. It is dates, how many people, which hotel, is visa included, is Jain food available. Your team answers the same ten questions for every enquiry, and most of those people are comparing three agents at once.

What the agent does for a travel business

  • Package questions: inclusions, hotels, meals, visa and what is not included, answered from the itineraries and price sheets you share.
  • Collects the details: destination, dates, number of travellers, budget and special needs, asked naturally in the chat.
  • Hands over a ready lead: your team opens the chat with everything already collected and sends the final quote.
  • Reads documents and photos: a customer sends a passport photo or a payment screenshot; the agent reads it and keeps it with the chat.
  • Follow-ups: a traveller who got a quote and went quiet gets a nudge after a delay you set, and it stops the moment they reply.
  • Season offers: send a Diwali or summer-holiday package to your list in one broadcast; the agent answers every reply.

The agent doesn't issue tickets or confirm fares. Fares and availability change by the hour, so the final quote and the booking stay with your team. The agent does the answering, the collecting and the chasing.

How it works

  1. Connect your WhatsApp number. Your existing number, connected through Meta's official WhatsApp Cloud API.
  2. Send us what you already have. Catalog, price list, brochure, menu or fee sheet. A PDF is fine.
  3. The agent takes the chats. We test it on real conversations first. Then it answers day and night, and your team steps in only when needed.

Frequently asked questions

Can it answer tour package questions on WhatsApp?

Yes. It answers from the itineraries, price sheets and brochures you share with us. If something isn't in your material, it brings in your team instead of guessing.

Does it book flights or hotels?

No. It collects the traveller's details and hands the lead to your team, who send the final quote and make the booking.

Does it follow up with people who asked for a quote?

Yes. After a delay you set it sends a nudge, within WhatsApp's template rules, and stops the moment the customer replies.

Can my staff still reply?

Yes. Every chat is in a shared inbox. Anyone on your team can take over a conversation with one tap; the agent steps back until you hand it back.

Is my WhatsApp number safe?

Yes. Wogent uses Meta's official WhatsApp Cloud API with your own business account, not unofficial automation tools. Your account still follows WhatsApp's Business and messaging policies.
