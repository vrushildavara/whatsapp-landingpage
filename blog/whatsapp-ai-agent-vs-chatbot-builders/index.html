Managed WhatsApp AI agent vs DIY platforms like WATI, Interakt and AiSensy

13 October 2026 6 min read

WATI, Interakt and AiSensy are self-serve WhatsApp Business API platforms: you sign up, start a free trial and build chatbots and AI agents yourself in their tools. Wogent is a managed WhatsApp AI agent: the Wogent team connects your number, loads your catalog, tests the agent on real conversations and keeps it running, with a focus on Hindi, Gujarati and English. Which is better depends on whether you want to build it yourself or have it built for you.

What each one says it is

Based on each company's own website, checked on 13 October 2026:

  • WATI describes itself as an AI-powered customer engagement platform, offers a free trial, lists no-code chatbots, and promotes "Astra" AI agents. It shows an "Official Meta Business Partner" badge.
  • Interakt describes itself as "a full-stack growth engine for marketing, sales, and support" on the WhatsApp Business Platform, offers a free trial and a drag-and-drop no-code chatbot builder, and says you can deploy AI agents.
  • AiSensy describes itself as an AI-powered WhatsApp marketing platform and an official WhatsApp Business Solution Partner, offers a free trial and a free plan, and promotes no-code chatbots and AI agents.
  • Wogent is a managed WhatsApp AI agent for Indian SMBs, built on the official WhatsApp Cloud API and set up for you rather than DIY.

Features change often; check each vendor's site for the latest.

When a self-serve platform is the better choice

  • You have a marketing or ops person who enjoys building flows
  • You need many channels or deep marketing automation
  • You want to start for free and experiment

When a managed AI agent is the better choice

  • Nobody on your team has time to build and maintain a bot
  • Your customers write in Hindi, Gujarati or a mix, and you want that handled well from day one
  • You want orders, bookings and follow-ups working, tested on your real chats, before you rely on it

Frequently asked questions

Is Wogent a WATI or AiSensy alternative?

It's a different approach. WATI, Interakt and AiSensy are self-serve platforms you set up yourself. Wogent is a managed WhatsApp AI agent that the Wogent team sets up and maintains for you, focused on Hindi, Gujarati and English.

Do WATI, Interakt and AiSensy have AI agents?

According to their own websites (checked 13 October 2026), all three promote AI agents or AI chatbots alongside no-code builders.

Which is better for a small shop with no tech team?

Usually a managed service, because someone else does the setup, testing and updates. If you have time to build and maintain flows yourself, a self-serve platform can work well.
