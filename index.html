Before you book.

Is my WhatsApp number safe?

Yes. WatsAgent AI uses the official WhatsApp Cloud API with your business account. We don't use unofficial WhatsApp automation tools. Your account remains subject to WhatsApp's Business and messaging policies.

How long does setup take?

Usually a few days. We connect your number, load your documents and systems, and test it on real conversations before it goes live.

Does it follow up when a customer goes quiet?

Yes. After a delay you set, it sends a nudge and stops the moment they reply, always within WhatsApp's template rules.

How much does it cost?

A flat monthly plan based on your message volume and number of WhatsApp numbers. Book a demo and we'll quote it.
