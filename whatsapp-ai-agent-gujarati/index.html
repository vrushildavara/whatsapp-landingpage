A WhatsApp AI agent that replies in Gujarati

Updated 13 October 2026

Wogent is a WhatsApp AI agent that understands and replies in Gujarati: in Gujarati script, in Gujarati typed with English letters ("stock ma che?"), and in messages that mix Gujarati, Hindi and English. It answers in whichever language the customer uses, takes orders and books appointments, and is set up for you on the official WhatsApp Cloud API.

Your customers don't write in textbook Gujarati

Real customer messages look like "bhai LED bulb 9W wala che? 10 joiye che, price su che aapna". Script-based chatbots break on messages like this. An AI agent reads what the customer means and answers it.

What "replies in Gujarati" means

  • Gujarati script (ગુજરાતી) and Gujarati in English letters are both understood.
  • Mixed messages, e.g. Gujarati with Hindi and English words, are normal and handled.
  • It follows the customer: it replies in the language they wrote in, and switches if they switch.
  • Your catalog can stay in English. The agent answers in Gujarati from an English price list or brochure.
  • Voice notes too: it listens to voice messages, and can reply with a voice note in Hindi or Hinglish.

Wogent supports three languages: Gujarati, Hindi and English. It's built for businesses in Ahmedabad, Surat, Rajkot, Vadodara and anywhere else customers write in Gujarati.

How it works

  1. Connect your WhatsApp number. Your existing number, connected through Meta's official WhatsApp Cloud API.
  2. Send us what you already have. Catalog, price list, brochure, menu or fee sheet. A PDF is fine.
  3. The agent takes the chats. We test it on real conversations first. Then it answers day and night, and your team steps in only when needed.

Frequently asked questions

Does Wogent reply in Gujarati?

Yes. It understands Gujarati in Gujarati script and in English letters, and replies in Gujarati when the customer writes in Gujarati.

Can it handle Gujarati, Hindi and English in one message?

Yes. Mixed-language messages are normal for Indian customers. The agent understands them and replies in the customer's language.

Do I need my catalog in Gujarati?

No. The agent can answer in Gujarati from a catalog or price list written in English.

Which languages does Wogent support?

Gujarati, Hindi and English, including any mix of the three.

Is my WhatsApp number safe?

Yes. Wogent uses Meta's official WhatsApp Cloud API with your own business account, not unofficial automation tools. Your account still follows WhatsApp's Business and messaging policies.

How much does it cost?

There is no monthly fee. You recharge a prepaid wallet and pay per use: ₹1 per AI reply, 5 paise per utility template, 10 paise on top of Meta's charge per marketing template, and human replies are free. New accounts get 2,000 free credits. Full rate card at web.wogent.in/pricing.
