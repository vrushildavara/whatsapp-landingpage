A WhatsApp AI agent that replies in Hindi

Updated 13 October 2026

Wogent is a WhatsApp AI agent that understands and replies in Hindi: in Devanagari, in Hinglish typed with English letters ("price kya hai?"), and in messages that mix Hindi with English or Gujarati. It can also listen to voice notes and reply with a voice note in a natural Hindi or Hinglish voice.

Hindi on WhatsApp is mostly Hinglish

Most customers type Hindi in English letters, mix in English words and skip punctuation: "bhaiya ye wala kitne ka hai, aaj deliver ho jayega?". The agent reads messages the way a person at your counter would.

What "replies in Hindi" means

  • Devanagari and Hinglish are both understood.
  • Voice notes both ways: it listens to voice messages and can answer with a voice note in a natural Hindi or Hinglish voice, when the customer asks, when they sent one, or always. Prices, links and order numbers follow as text.
  • It follows the customer: Hindi in, Hindi out; English in, English out; mixed in, mixed out.
  • Your catalog can stay in English. The agent answers in Hindi from an English price list or brochure.

Wogent supports three languages: Hindi, Gujarati and English, including any mix of them.

How it works

  1. Connect your WhatsApp number. Your existing number, connected through Meta's official WhatsApp Cloud API.
  2. Send us what you already have. Catalog, price list, brochure, menu or fee sheet. A PDF is fine.
  3. The agent takes the chats. We test it on real conversations first. Then it answers day and night, and your team steps in only when needed.

Frequently asked questions

Does Wogent reply in Hindi?

Yes. It understands Hindi in Devanagari and in Hinglish, and replies in Hindi when the customer writes in Hindi.

Can it send voice notes in Hindi?

Yes. It can reply with a WhatsApp voice note in a natural Hindi or Hinglish voice, when the customer asks, when they sent a voice note, or always, as you prefer.

Do I need my catalog in Hindi?

No. The agent answers in Hindi from a catalog or price list written in English.

Which languages does Wogent support?

Hindi, Gujarati and English, including any mix of the three.

Is my WhatsApp number safe?

Yes. Wogent uses Meta's official WhatsApp Cloud API with your own business account, not unofficial automation tools. Your account still follows WhatsApp's Business and messaging policies.

A flat monthly plan based on your message volume and number of WhatsApp numbers. Book a free demo and we'll quote it.
