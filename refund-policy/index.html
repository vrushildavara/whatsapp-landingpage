Refund and Cancellation Policy

Last updated 23 November 2026

You can cancel a Wogent plan at any time, and it ends at the end of the period you already paid for. Payments for a period that has started and wallet credits are not refundable, except for failed sends, duplicate or wrong charges, and where the law requires. Approved refunds go back to your original payment method within 5 to 7 working days.

1. Cancelling a plan

  • You can cancel a monthly or yearly plan at any time from Plans and billing in your dashboard, or by writing to us.
  • Cancelling stops future renewals. Your plan and its features stay active until the end of the period you paid for; after that your workspace moves to pay as you go, and you can keep using any paid wallet credits.
  • We do not refund the rest of a period that has already started, whether monthly or yearly.

2. Changing plans

  • Upgrading starts the higher plan at once. The unused part of your current paid period is added to your wallet as paid credits, which do not expire. It is not refunded in cash.
  • Downgrading or switching between monthly and yearly takes effect at the end of your current period; nothing is charged until then.

3. Failed renewals

If a renewal payment fails, Razorpay retries it and your plan stays active for 3 more days. If it still has not been paid, your workspace moves to pay as you go. Nothing is charged for the time without a plan.

4. Wallet credits

  • Paid credits do not expire and can be used at any time while your account is open.
  • Credits for messages that fail to send are not charged, and a template that WhatsApp reports as failed is credited back automatically.
  • Otherwise, wallet recharges and the credits included with plans are not refundable or transferable, and bonus, trial and reward credits have no cash value.

5. When we refund

  • You were charged twice for the same recharge or renewal, or charged an amount different from the one shown at checkout.
  • Money left your account but the payment failed, or the credits or plan you paid for were not added to your workspace and we could not fix it.
  • We end the service for a reason that is not your fault; we then refund any paid credits you have not used.
  • Any other case where the law requires a refund.

6. How to ask for a refund

Write to wogent7@gmail.com within 30 days of the payment, with your registered email, the payment date and amount, and the Razorpay payment ID or invoice number. We reply within 2 working days.

Approved refunds are made to the original payment method through Razorpay within 5 to 7 working days. Your bank may take a few more days to show it.
