WhatsApp AI agent for businesses in Surat

Updated 16 November 2026

Wogent is a WhatsApp AI agent from Surat, Gujarat, for businesses such as textile and saree traders, retail shops, clinics and coaching classes. It answers customers, takes orders and books appointments 24/7 in Gujarati, Hindi and English, and is set up for you on the official WhatsApp Cloud API. The team works remotely, so setup and support happen online, for Surat and the rest of India.

Surat does business on WhatsApp

A saree wholesaler sends catalog photos to buyers in other states. A shop in Adajan gets "stock ma che?" forty times a day. A dental clinic on Ghod Dod Road gets slot requests at midnight. Almost all of it comes in on WhatsApp, in Gujarati typed with English letters, and most of it waits until someone is free to reply.

What it does for a Surat business

  • Replies in Gujarati the way Surat types it: "ketla nu che?", "stock ma che?", Gujarati script, Hindi and English, even mixed in one message.
  • Answers from your catalog: rates, designs, colours and stock from the price list or catalog you already send to buyers.
  • Takes the order in the chat: quantity, rate, confirmed, at any hour.
  • Reads photos: a buyer forwards a design photo; the agent finds it and quotes it.
  • Books appointments: for clinics, salons and classes.
  • Sends festival offers: one Diwali or Uttarayan broadcast to your whole list, with every reply answered.
  • Your staff take over when needed: every chat sits in a shared inbox.

A Gujarati-speaking team, online

Wogent started in Surat and works remotely. We set the agent up for you over a call and WhatsApp, test it on your real chats and speak to you in Gujarati or Hindi, with no office visit needed. You can also sign up and try it yourself with free credits.

How it works

  1. Connect your WhatsApp number. Your existing number, connected through Meta's official WhatsApp Cloud API.
  2. Send us what you already have. Catalog, price list, brochure, menu or fee sheet. A PDF is fine.
  3. The agent takes the chats. We test it on real conversations first. Then it answers day and night, and your team steps in only when needed.

Frequently asked questions

Is Wogent based in Surat?

Yes. Wogent is from Surat, Gujarat. The team works remotely, so setup and support happen online for businesses anywhere in India.

Does it reply in Gujarati?

Yes. It understands Gujarati script and Gujarati typed in English letters, such as "ketla nu che?", and replies in the language the customer uses.

Which Surat businesses is it for?

Any business that gets customers on WhatsApp: textile and saree traders, retail and wholesale shops, clinics, salons, restaurants, coaching classes and real estate.

Can my staff still reply?

Yes. Every chat is in a shared inbox. Anyone on your team can take over a conversation with one tap; the agent steps back until you hand it back.

Is my WhatsApp number safe?

Yes. Wogent uses Meta's official WhatsApp Cloud API with your own business account, not unofficial automation tools. Your account still follows WhatsApp's Business and messaging policies.

How much does it cost?

There is no monthly fee. You recharge a prepaid wallet and pay per use: ₹0.80 per AI reply, 5 paise per utility template, 10 paise on top of Meta's charge per marketing template, and human replies are free. New accounts get 2,000 free credits. Full rate card at web.wogent.in/pricing.
