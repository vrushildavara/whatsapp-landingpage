What it actually does

Not a chatbot script. A WhatsApp agent that runs your front desk.

01

Understands Hindi, Gujarati and English — often in the same message

Your customers don't switch languages to talk to a machine. WhatsAgent AI reads mixed Hindi-Gujarati-English messages the way people actually type them, and replies in the same language and tone.

02

Answers straight from your product catalog

Upload your price list, stock, or service menu once. When a customer asks "is this in stock" or "what's the price", the agent answers from your real catalog — not a guess.

03

Takes the order or books the appointment — right in the chat

No redirect to a form or app. The agent collects quantity, confirms price, and closes the order or appointment inside the same WhatsApp conversation.

04

Remembers context across the conversation

A returning customer doesn't repeat themselves. WhatsAgent AI recalls what they asked before, what they ordered, and where things were left off.

05

Hands off to a human when it should

An upset customer, an unusual request, or something outside what it knows — the agent recognizes it and brings in your team, with the full conversation already in front of them.

06

Feels like a real person, not a bot

It sees the message the moment it lands, shows "typing…" while it composes a reply, and can react to a message with an emoji — the same as anyone would on WhatsApp. No robotic instant replies.

07

Follows up when a customer goes quiet — so you don't have to remember

Someone asks a price, then disappears. After a delay you decide, the agent sends a nudge — a fixed message, one of your approved WhatsApp templates, or a short note it writes from the conversation — and stops the moment they reply. If the chat has been silent for more than a day, it switches to an approved template on its own, so every follow-up stays within WhatsApp's rules.

Questions

Before you book a demo.

Is my WhatsApp number safe?

Yes. WhatsAgent AI connects through Meta's official WhatsApp Cloud API — the same platform WhatsApp itself provides for businesses. Your number stays yours, and you can disconnect it at any time.

Which languages does it support?

Hindi, Gujarati, and English today — including the mixed, code-switched way Indian customers actually type. The agent detects the language per message and replies in kind.

How long does setup take?

Most businesses are live within a day. We connect your WhatsApp number, load your catalog or service list, and set the agent's tone — you review a test conversation before it goes live.

Can it use my product catalog?

Yes. Share your price list, stock sheet, or service menu and the agent answers from it directly — prices, availability, and specs included, not generic guesses.

What happens when a customer needs a human?

The agent recognizes complaints, unusual requests, or anything outside what it knows, and hands the conversation to your team with full context — no repeating themselves.

Does it follow up with customers who stop replying?

Yes. If a customer asks something and then goes quiet, the agent follows up after a delay you decide — once, or a few times spaced out — and stops as soon as they reply. It respects WhatsApp's 24-hour rule: within a day of the customer's last message it can send a normal reply; after that it uses one of your approved templates.

How much does it cost?

Pricing depends on your message volume and how many WhatsApp numbers you run. Book a demo and we'll quote a flat monthly plan for your business.
