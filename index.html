Before you book.

Is my WhatsApp number safe?

Yes. Wogent uses the official WhatsApp Cloud API with your business account. We don't use unofficial WhatsApp automation tools. Your account remains subject to WhatsApp's Business and messaging policies.

How long does setup take?

Usually a few days. We connect your number, load your documents and systems, and test it on real conversations before it goes live.

Does it follow up when a customer goes quiet?

Yes. After a delay you set, it sends a nudge and stops the moment they reply, always within WhatsApp's template rules. For longer sequences, like a welcome series or a reminder the next day and again a week later, use a journey.

What is a journey?

A set of WhatsApp messages that runs on its own for each customer. You choose what starts it, such as a first message, a keyword like OFFER or a new tag, then the messages, the waits and what happens if they reply. It stops when they reply, buy, unsubscribe or your team takes over the chat.

Can I send an offer to only some customers?

Yes. Send to a list you upload, to a saved group such as "bought in the last 90 days", or to everyone with a tag. Schedule it for later, and afterwards send again only to the people who didn't read it or didn't reply.

Will it message people who said STOP?

No. When someone replies STOP, they are taken off your broadcasts and journeys straight away. If they reply START, they are back on.

How much does it cost?

There is no monthly fee. You recharge a prepaid wallet and pay per use: ₹1 per AI reply, 5 paise per utility template and 10 paise per marketing template on top of Meta's own charge. Replies from your team are free, and new accounts get 2,000 free credits. See the full rate card.

What languages does the WhatsApp AI agent support?

Hindi, Gujarati and English, including mixed messages where a customer switches between all three in one chat. The agent replies in whichever language the customer uses.

Can it understand and send WhatsApp voice notes?

Yes, both ways. When a customer sends a voice note, the agent listens to it and replies to what they said. It can also reply with a voice note of its own in a natural Hindi or Hinglish voice: when the customer asks for one, when they sent one, or always, as you prefer. Prices, links and order numbers follow as text so they're easy to copy.

Is this a DIY chatbot builder or a managed service?

Managed service. Wogent's team connects your WhatsApp number, loads your catalog or documents, configures the agent and tests it on real conversations before it goes live. We set up your first broadcasts and journeys with you too. You never have to build anything, though you can change a message or a wait yourself whenever you like.

Which businesses is Wogent built for?

Indian small and medium businesses that get customers on WhatsApp, including retail shops, clinics, real estate agents, restaurants, education businesses and service businesses. If your customers already message you on WhatsApp, Wogent can handle those conversations.

Can my team still see and reply to chats?

Yes. Every conversation appears in a shared inbox. Any team member can take over a chat with one tap, and the agent steps back immediately and stays out until you hand it back.

Can I control what each staff member can do?

Yes. Invite your staff by email as an admin, agent or viewer. Agents can reply to chats, viewers can only read, and admins manage the team, broadcasts and settings. Every change is recorded in an activity log.

What happens if something goes down?

It keeps replying. Each outside service the agent depends on has its own timeout and fallback, so one failure never stops your WhatsApp. No customer message is lost: if a reply is interrupted, the agent picks it up again and answers.

Does it work with my existing WhatsApp number?

Yes. Wogent connects your existing WhatsApp Business number through Meta's official WhatsApp Cloud API. You keep your number and your chat history.

What is the WhatsApp Cloud API?

It is Meta's official API for WhatsApp Business. It lets businesses send and receive WhatsApp messages programmatically, at scale, without using unofficial tools that risk account bans. Wogent is built on this API.
