How to send WhatsApp broadcasts without risking your number

16 November 2026 6 min read

Business numbers get restricted when they send bulk messages through unofficial tools, or when many recipients block and report them. To broadcast with far less risk, send through Meta's official WhatsApp Business API, message only people who agreed to hear from you, use templates Meta has approved, give everyone a working way to stop, and watch your quality rating. No tool can promise a number will never be restricted; these rules are what keep the risk low.

Why numbers get restricted

  • Unofficial bulk senders. Software that drives the normal WhatsApp app to message thousands of contacts breaks WhatsApp's terms. This is the most common reason a shop loses its number.
  • Messaging strangers. Bought lists and scraped numbers produce blocks and reports within minutes.
  • Too much, too often. Even real customers block a business that messages every day.

1. Use the official WhatsApp Business API

The free WhatsApp Business app has a broadcast list, but it is small and only reaches people who have saved your number. For anything larger, use a product built on Meta's official WhatsApp Business API. It is the only approved way to message customers at scale.

2. Message only people who opted in

WhatsApp requires that a person has agreed to receive messages from your business. In practice: customers who messaged you first, people who ticked a box at checkout, people who gave their number at the counter and were told they would get offers on WhatsApp. Never upload a list you bought.

3. Use approved templates, in the right category

A message you start must be a template that Meta has approved. Offers and greetings are marketing templates; order updates and reminders are utility templates. Putting an offer inside a utility template gets it rejected or reclassified. Keep the wording clear, name your business, and personalise it with the customer's name.

4. Make STOP work

Tell people how to stop, and remove them the moment they ask. A customer who can't unsubscribe will block and report you instead, and that is what damages a number.

5. Respect the clock and the calendar

  • Don't send at night. Hold messages until morning.
  • Don't send to the same person every day. Once or twice a week is plenty for most shops.
  • Don't send the same offer to everyone. A tagged group that actually buys that product will read it.

6. Watch your quality rating

Meta gives every business number a quality rating based on how recipients react, and it limits how many new people you can message in a day. Good quality raises that limit over time; a run of blocks lowers it. If your rating drops, stop broadcasting, tighten your list and wait for it to recover.

7. Answer the replies

A broadcast that gets replies is a conversation, and conversations are what WhatsApp is for. A broadcast that gets silence and blocks looks like spam. Make sure someone, or something, answers every reply quickly.

A pre-send checklist

  1. Is this list made only of people who agreed to hear from us?
  2. Is the template approved, in the right category?
  3. Does the message say who we are and how to stop?
  4. Is it going out in daytime?
  5. Is someone ready to answer the replies?
  6. Do we know what this broadcast will cost at Meta's rate?

Frequently asked questions

Why did my WhatsApp Business number get banned after sending bulk messages?

Most often because the messages were sent with an unofficial bulk tool, which breaks WhatsApp's terms, or because many recipients blocked or reported the number. Sending to bought or scraped lists causes both.

Is it allowed to send bulk WhatsApp messages to customers?

Sending through Meta's official WhatsApp Business API to people who have opted in is allowed by WhatsApp's policies. Messaging people who never agreed to hear from you breaks those policies.

How many people can I broadcast to on WhatsApp?

On the official API, Meta sets a daily limit on how many new people each business can message. The limit rises as you keep a good quality rating and falls if many people block you.

Can any tool guarantee my number won't be banned?

No. Be wary of anyone who promises that. The official API, opted-in contacts, approved templates and a working STOP keep the risk low, but your account always has to follow WhatsApp's policies.
