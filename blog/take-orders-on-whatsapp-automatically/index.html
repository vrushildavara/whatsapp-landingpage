How to take orders on WhatsApp automatically

16 November 2026 6 min read

There are three ways to take orders on WhatsApp without typing every reply yourself. The free WhatsApp Business app gives you a catalog and a cart, but a person still has to confirm each order. A menu chatbot on the WhatsApp Business API takes orders through fixed buttons. An AI agent on the API reads normal messages such as "2 geyser bhej do", checks your price list, confirms the order and follows up, at any hour.

Option 1: the WhatsApp Business app catalog

The free WhatsApp Business app lets you add products with photos and prices. Customers can browse the catalog, add items to a cart and send the cart to you as a message. It costs nothing and takes an afternoon to set up.

The limit is that the cart is only a message. Someone on your side still has to read it, check stock, confirm the total and reply. At 11 PM or during a rush, that reply waits.

Option 2: a menu chatbot

On the WhatsApp Business API you can build a flow: the customer taps "Order", picks a category, picks a product, picks a quantity. It works at any hour and the answers are predictable.

The limit is that customers don't write in menus. They write "bhaiya kal wala hi bhej do" or send a photo of a handwritten list. A menu bot either ignores that or sends the menu again.

Option 3: an AI agent

An AI agent reads the message the way your staff would. It works out what the customer wants, looks up the product and price in your catalog, asks for anything missing, and confirms the order in the chat.

What you need before you automate orders

  1. A clean price list. A PDF or sheet with product names, variants and prices. If two prices disagree, fix that first.
  2. A rule for stock. Either the agent checks your system, or you tell it what is always available and what needs confirming.
  3. A rule for payment and delivery. Advance or COD, delivery area and charges, written down.
  4. Someone to take over. Bulk orders, complaints and special requests should reach a person with the chat already open.
  5. A number on the WhatsApp Business API. Your existing business number can usually be moved.

Which option should you pick?

  • A few orders a day, during shop hours: the free Business app is enough.
  • A short, fixed menu: a menu chatbot works and is predictable.
  • Many orders, messy messages, late-night enquiries: an AI agent pays for itself in the orders you currently miss.

Frequently asked questions

Can I take orders on WhatsApp for free?

Yes, with the free WhatsApp Business app's catalog and cart. A person still has to read and confirm each order, so it only works while someone is available to reply.

Can customers order on WhatsApp without filling a form?

Yes. An AI agent takes the order from normal chat messages, asks for anything missing such as quantity or address, and confirms it in the same chat.

Do I need the WhatsApp Business API to automate orders?

Yes for any real automation. The free Business app only offers quick replies, away messages and a catalog; chatbots and AI agents run on the WhatsApp Business API.

Can an AI agent read a photo of a handwritten order list?

Yes. Wogent's agent reads photos customers send, including product photos, payment screenshots and handwritten lists.
